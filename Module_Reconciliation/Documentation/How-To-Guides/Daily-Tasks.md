# Daily Tasks

Last Updated: 2026-11-12

## Clear one day

1. Open **Receiving Validator** from the Reporting group.
2. Choose **Work a Receiving Day**.
3. Leave the day on today, or pick the day you want.
4. Read the summary strip: how many lines were scanned, kept, changed, unresolved, skipped or not found.
5. Work the review cycle with the keyboard: the destination is already filled in from the movement evidence. Press **Enter** to accept it, or **Escape** to skip the line for this run.
6. When nothing is left to review, the day is marked complete for the session.

## Change a destination before accepting

Type over the destination in the detail pane, then press Enter. The line is written with your location and marked **Manually Set**.

## Deal with the lines the module could not answer

Press **Needs Attention**. It is enabled only when something is waiting. Each line shows why it could not be answered, the evidence behind it, and the raw difference. Type a location for a line and set it; the write goes through the same path as an accepted line.

## Clear a backlog

Set a start and end day in the sweep controls and choose **Start sweep**. Each day is worked through the same single-day pass. The sweep stops on the first day that fails and leaves you on it, so you can see what went wrong. **Stop** ends the sweep between days.

## Move between days

Use **Previous** and **Next** to move to the nearest day that has receiving rows. **Today** brings you back in one press.

## Filter the grid

Filter by outcome with the outcome filter, and by part or purchase order with the text filter. The filters narrow what you see; they do not change what the day counted.
