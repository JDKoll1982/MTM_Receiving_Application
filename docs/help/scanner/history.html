🎓 End-User Help

Scanner History

History is a read-only view of scanner lists that have already been worked on. Use it to answer the question “what did we move, and when?”

What History Shows

The History tab lists past scanner sessions. Selecting one shows the individual lines that were in it — part number, source location, and destination.

  • History is reference only. Nothing on this tab can be edited or re-sent.
  • To move material again, go back to the Workbench and build a new list.
  • Your current list is not in History — it stays on the Workbench until you clear it.
Filters on top · past sessions on the left · the selected session’s lines on the right

Filtering the List

History opens with a sensible default range so it is never showing everything at once. Four controls sit across the top:

Control What it does
From The earliest session date to include.
To The latest session date to include.
Status Narrows the list to one session status, or leave it blank for all.
Refresh Reloads the list using the filters you have set.
Clear filters Resets the dates and the status back to their defaults and reloads.
ℹ️
Looking for something older? Widen the From date. The default range only covers recent sessions, so older lists are hidden until you extend the range.
✅
When a refresh finishes, the message strip confirms how many records were loaded — for example “Loaded 12 scanner history records.”

Sessions and Lines

The lower half of the screen splits into two lists that work together.

🗂️
Sessions (left)
One entry per past scanner list, showing its name, its final status, and when it was created.
📄
Items (right)
The lines belonging to whichever session you have selected on the left.

Click a session on the left and its lines appear on the right. Nothing happens until you select a session, so the right-hand list starts empty.

Reading a Past List

Each line in the Items list shows the same four things you saw on the Workbench, in the same order:

  1. Line number — its position in that session.
  2. Part — the part number that was moved.
  3. From — the source location.
  4. To — the destination location.
⚠️
History does not include a quantity column. If you need to know how many moved, check the Infor Visual transaction records.

Common Questions

The list is empty.
Widen the From date and press Refresh. If it is still empty, no scanner sessions have been recorded in that period.
My current list is not shown here.
Correct — the list you are working on lives on the Workbench. History shows sessions that have already been completed.
Can I re-send a line from History?
No. History is read-only. Go to the Workbench, look the part or location up again, and build a fresh line.
Can I export the history list?
The Export button belongs to the Workbench and writes out your current list. History itself is for reading on screen.
