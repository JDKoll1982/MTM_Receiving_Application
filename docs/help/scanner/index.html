Scanner Workbench

The Scanner module moves material between locations in Infor Visual. You build a short list of lines — each line says which part, from where, to where, and how many — and then send the lines one at a time while Infor Visual is open.

Overview

Scanner is a transfer tool. It does not receive material and it does not change quantities on a Purchase Order. It records that a quantity of a part moved from one warehouse location to another.

The Scanner Workbench — entry bar at the top, the current list in the middle, actions at the bottom
ℹ️
Everything you see on this screen is one list. The app saves that list as you go, so if you close the app and come back later, the lines you had not sent yet are still waiting for you.

Who Can Use Scanner

Scanner is restricted. If your employee number is not on the approved list, the Scanner button does not appear in the left-hand navigation bar and the keyboard shortcut does nothing.

  • The approved list is maintained plant-wide by an administrator.
  • It is managed from Core Settings ▸ Scanner Access.
  • Changes apply to every workstation — there is nothing to install.
  • Access is re-checked each time you open Scanner, so a change takes effect immediately.
⚠️
Scanner is missing from your navigation? Your employee number is not on the allow-list. Ask a supervisor or an administrator to add you in Core Settings ▸ Scanner Access.

The Three Tabs

The Scanner screen has three tabs across the top. You can switch between them at any time without losing the list you are working on.

🖥️
Workbench
The list you are working on right now. Add lines, fill in destinations, and send them to Infor Visual.
🕘
History
Past scanner lists and the lines they contained. Read-only reference.
⚙️
Settings
The Infor Visual profiles Scanner uses — window titles, defaults, and the send shortcut.
🖥️ The Workbench Part search, location search, ranges, the table, and sending. 🕘 History Find a past list and read the lines it contained. ⚙️ Profiles & Settings Configure how Scanner talks to Infor Visual.

The Transfer Workflow

Every transfer follows the same five steps. The first three happen in the app, the last two happen in Infor Visual.

1

Find the material

Search by part number, or switch to searching by location if you would rather start from a shelf.

2

Pick the lines to move

A picker window opens with the parts and their on-hand quantities. Tick the rows you want and choose Use Selected. Each pick becomes one line in the current list.

3

Fill in the destination

Type the To location for each line. Scanner tidies up the location text for you and tells you straight away whether the line is ready. When every line is green, the list is ready.

4

Send the line

Select a green line and press the blue Send button. Scanner switches to Infor Visual and fills in the transfer for you.

5

Confirm it saved

Scanner asks “Was the transaction saved in Infor Visual?” Answer Yes to clear the line from your list, or No to keep it and try again.

✅
One line at a time. Scanner sends the line you selected, not the whole list. This keeps the Infor Visual entries reliable and lets you confirm each save.

About Locations

Location names in Infor Visual come in a few shapes. Scanner recognises all of them and rewrites what you type into one standard form, so you do not have to remember the exact spelling.

You type Scanner uses
va001  or  VA001  or  Va001V-A0-01
V-A001  or  VA0-01V-A0-01
VA-A001  or  VAA001VA-A0-01
r04  or  R04  or  R4R-04
s00  or  S0S-00
w04  or  W-4W-04
RECVRECV  (left as typed)
ℹ️
If what you typed does not match any known pattern, Scanner leaves it exactly as you typed it. Transfers always move material within the same warehouse (002); there is nothing to choose.

Common Questions

Does Scanner change the quantity on hand?
It moves quantity from one location to another. The total quantity for the part does not change — only where the material is recorded as being.
I closed the app. Did I lose my list?
No. The current list is saved as you work. Reopen Scanner and the lines you had not sent are still there.
Can I work on more than one list at a time?
No. Scanner keeps one current list. If you want to start over, use Clear list and then add new lines.
Why is the Scanner button missing from my navigation?
Scanner access is limited to an approved list of employee numbers. Ask an administrator to add you in Core Settings ▸ Scanner Access.
Infor Visual opened but nothing was filled in.
Scanner needs Infor Visual to be at the window named in its profile. Check Profiles & Settings, or ask a developer if the Infor Visual window title has changed.
