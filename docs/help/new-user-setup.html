New User Setup

The first time you use the MTM Receiving Application on a new computer, the app automatically opens the New User Entry form so it can create a profile for you. This page walks through every field so you can complete the form quickly and correctly.

When does this appear?

The New User Entry form opens automatically at the end of the startup sequence when the app cannot find an existing account for your Windows username. This happens in two situations:

  • You are logging in to this computer for the first time — no profile exists yet.
  • You have been issued a new Windows username — the old profile won't match the new login.
ℹ️
You only need to do this once per computer. Once your account is created, the app will recognise you on every subsequent launch and skip this form entirely.

Before You Start

Have the following information ready before filling out the form:

Information needed Where to find it Required?
Your first and last name Your employee badge or your supervisor Required
Employee number Your pay stub, badge, or HR — e.g., 6229 Required
Your department Your supervisor — select from the dropdown or enter a custom name Required
Your shift Your work schedule (e.g., Day, Afternoon, Night) Required
4-digit PIN Choose a PIN yourself — used for shared terminal login Required
InforVisual (ERP) username & password Provided by IT — the same credentials used to log in to Infor Visual Required

The Form at a Glance

Here is the complete New User Entry form. The sections are arranged in two columns on the top half and one wide column at the bottom. Work through each section from left to right, top to bottom.

Step 1 — Name

The Name section is in the top-left of the form. Enter your legal first and last name exactly as it appears on your employee badge.

Type your First Name and Last Name

Click the First Name field (highlighted yellow in the screenshot) and type your first name. Press Tab to move to Last Name and type your surname.

Enter your first and last name in the highlighted fields

Step 2 — Employee Details

The Employee Details section sits below the Name section. It has two fields: Employee Number (which you type) and Windows Username (which is filled in automatically).

2

Enter your Employee Number

Type your employee number (for example, 6229) into the Employee Number field. This number appears on your pay stub, badge, or can be provided by HR.

Enter your employee number — the Windows Username is filled in automatically
ℹ️
Windows Username is read-only. The app detects your current Windows login and fills in the Windows Username field automatically. You cannot change it — this is the username that will be used to recognise you on future logins.

Step 3 — Department Assignment

The Department Assignment section is directly below Employee Details. Select your department from the dropdown list.

3

Select your Department

Click the Department dropdown (highlighted yellow) and choose your department from the list.

Click the Department dropdown and select your department

Don't see your department? Select "Other"

If your department is not in the list, choose Other. A new Custom Department Name field appears (highlighted yellow below) — type your department name there.

When "Other" is selected, type your department name in the Custom Department Name field

Step 4 — Work Schedule

The Work Schedule section is in the top-right of the form, opposite the Name section. It has a single Shift dropdown.

4

Select your Shift

Click the Shift dropdown (highlighted yellow) and choose your normal working shift — for example, Day, Afternoon, or Night. If you are unsure, ask your supervisor.

Select your shift from the dropdown

Step 5 — Account Security

The Account Security section is in the right column below Work Schedule. You create a 4-digit PIN here.

5

Create your 4-Digit PIN

Type a 4-digit PIN into the 4-Digit PIN field (highlighted yellow), then retype the same PIN in the Confirm PIN field. Both entries must match before the Create Account button becomes active.

Enter your chosen PIN in both fields — they must match
⚠️
Remember your PIN. This PIN is used when logging in on a shared terminal where multiple users share the same Windows login. Choose something memorable but do not reuse a PIN you use for banking or other personal accounts.
🔒
Your PIN is strongly encrypted. The app uses AES-256 encryption — the same industry standard used by banks and government systems — to scramble your PIN before saving it. The encrypted data is stored only on this computer, in your personal Windows user profile, and is tied to this machine. It cannot be read by other users on the same computer, cannot be transferred to another machine, and is never sent over the network. Nobody — including IT staff — can recover your PIN from the stored data.

Step 6 — ERP System Access

The ERP System Access section spans the full width at the bottom of the form. Enter the credentials you use to log in to Infor Visual (the ERP system).

6

Enter your Visual/Infor Username and Password

Type your Infor Visual username in the Visual/Infor Username field and your password in the Visual/Infor Password field (both highlighted yellow). These are the same credentials you use to log in to the Infor Visual desktop application.

Enter the same username and password you use in Infor Visual
🔒
Your credentials are strongly encrypted. The app uses AES-256 encryption — the same industry standard used by banks and government systems — to scramble your username and password before saving them. The encrypted data is stored only on this computer, in your personal Windows user profile, and is tied to this machine. It cannot be read by other users on the same computer, cannot be transferred to another machine, and is never sent over the network. Nobody — including IT staff — can recover your password from the stored data.

Creating the Account

Once all required fields are filled in, the Create Account button at the bottom-right becomes active (turns solid blue). Click it to save your profile.

  • Create Account (blue) — saves your profile and opens the main application window. You will not see this form again on this computer.
  • Cancel (grey) — closes the form without creating an account. The app will close and you will need to relaunch it. The form will reappear because your profile still does not exist.
✅
Create Account is still grey? Check that all required fields are filled in — Name, Employee Number, Department, Shift, PIN (with matching Confirm PIN), and Visual/Infor Username and Password. Any missing required field will keep the button disabled.

Common Questions

Do I fill this out every time I log in?
No. You only fill it out once per computer. After your account is created, the app recognises your Windows username on every launch and goes straight to the main window.
My Windows Username is already filled in — can I change it?
No. The Windows Username field is read-only. It is filled in automatically from your current Windows login. If the username shown is wrong, log out of Windows, log back in with the correct account, and then relaunch the app.
I don't know my employee number. What should I enter?
Check your pay stub, employee badge, or ask your supervisor or HR. The employee number is typically a 4–5 digit number (for example, 6229). Do not leave this field blank or enter a placeholder — it is used to match your records in the system.
My department isn't in the dropdown. What do I do?
Select Other from the dropdown. A Custom Department Name field will appear directly below the dropdown — type your department name there.
What is the PIN used for?
The 4-digit PIN is used on shared terminals where multiple employees log in using the same Windows account. Instead of each person needing their own Windows login, you identify yourself with your PIN. If you have your own dedicated Windows login, you may still be asked for the PIN in certain workflows — choose something you will remember.
I don't have my InforVisual credentials right now. Can I skip that section?
No — the ERP System Access section is required. The Create Account button will remain disabled until a username and password are entered. Contact your IT department or supervisor to obtain your Infor Visual credentials before completing setup.
The "Create Account" button is still greyed out even though I filled in everything.

Check the following:

  • Both the 4-Digit PIN and Confirm PIN fields must contain the same number. Even a one-digit difference will keep the button disabled.
  • Make sure First Name, Last Name, Employee Number, Department, and Shift are all filled in.
  • If you chose Other for department, the Custom Department Name field must not be blank.
