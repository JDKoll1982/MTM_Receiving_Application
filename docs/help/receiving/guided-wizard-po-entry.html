PO Entry — Guided Wizard

The first step of the Guided Wizard asks you to identify what you are receiving — either by entering a Purchase Order number or by looking up a part directly. This page covers both paths, the parts selection table, and what to do if you encounter a Quality Hold part.

Overview

The PO Entry screen is the first step of the Guided Wizard. It has two input modes that you can switch between freely:

📄

PO Entry mode

Type a Purchase Order number, click Load PO, then pick the part from the list.

🔍

Non-PO mode

Type a Part ID, click Look Up Part, then pick the part from the list.

Both paths end at the same Available Parts table, where you click the correct row and then hit Next to continue.

PO Entry screen — PO number loaded, Available Parts shown below

Receiving with a PO Number

Use this path when you have the Purchase Order number from your paperwork or the delivery documentation.

1

Enter the PO Number

Click the Purchase Order Number field and type the PO number from your paperwork (for example, PO-033349). The field is highlighted in yellow in the screenshot below.

Type the PO number, then click the Load PO button (pink arrow)
ℹ️
The Status line below the text box updates once the PO is loaded. Common statuses include Partially Received, Open, and Closed.
2

Click "Load PO"

Press Enter or click the blue Load PO button. A brief loading indicator appears while the app retrieves the PO from InforVisual. The Available Parts table then populates with every open line item on that PO.

Available Parts table populated — select a row to enable the Next button
3

Select a part and click "Next"

Click the row in the Available Parts table that matches the part you are receiving. The row turns blue when selected. The Next button becomes active — click it to continue to the next wizard step.

Row selected (blue) → Next button becomes active (pink circle)
⚠️
If a part row shows its Part ID in red, it is flagged for a Quality Hold. Read the Quality Hold section below before selecting it.

Receiving Without a PO (Non-PO)

If you are receiving material that does not have a Purchase Order — for example, a return shipment or a direct-issue part — use the Non-PO path. Click Switch to Non-PO at the top of the screen to switch modes.

Click Switch to Non-PO (pink circle) to enter Non-PO mode
1

Enter the Part ID

Type the Part ID (e.g. MMC-001 or MMF-456) into the Part Identifier field. The Package Type line below automatically updates to show the detected package category (such as Skids, Coils, Sheet Material, etc.) once you type a recognized part.

Type the Part ID in the highlighted field, then click Look Up Part
2

Click "Look Up Part"

Click the blue Look Up Part button (or press Enter). The app performs a search and populates the Available Parts table with matching results.

A loading indicator (green bar) shows while the app searches InforVisual
🔍
Part ID not an exact match? If the Part ID you typed isn't found exactly in InforVisual, the app automatically opens the Smart Part Search dialog — a list of similar parts so you can pick the correct one. This is normal and does not mean an error occurred. See the Smart Part Search guide for full details →
3

Select the matching part and click "Next"

The matching part appears in the Available Parts table (highlighted yellow in the screenshot). Click the row to select it (it turns blue), then click Next.

Part selected (blue) → click Next to continue
ℹ️
For Non-PO items the Line # column shows N/A because there is no PO line associated with the part.

To switch back to PO mode, click Switch to PO Entry at any time before clicking Next.

Reading the Available Parts Table

Once a PO is loaded (or a Non-PO part is looked up), the Available Parts table shows the matching parts. Here is what each column means:

Column What it shows Notes
Part ID The unique identifier for the part as it appears in InforVisual Shown in red if the part is on Quality Hold
Description The part description from InforVisual May be truncated for long descriptions
Remaining Quantity still outstanding on the PO (ordered minus already received) For Non-PO parts this reflects available stock
Qty Ordered Original quantity ordered on the PO line N/A for Non-PO items
Line # The PO line number from InforVisual Shows N/A for Non-PO items

Selecting a row

Click anywhere on a row to select it. The selected row turns blue and the Next button in the bottom toolbar becomes active. You can only select one row at a time.

ℹ️
If Next is still greyed out after clicking a row, check whether the part is on Quality Hold — you may need to complete the acknowledgment first. See the Quality Hold section.

Quality Hold Parts

Some parts are flagged in InforVisual as requiring a Quality Hold. The app detects these automatically and requires two acknowledgments before you can proceed. This cannot be bypassed.

How to identify a Quality Hold part

Quality Hold parts appear in the Available Parts table with their Part ID shown in red text.

The Quality Hold part (MMFSR0001) is displayed in red in the parts table

The Quality Hold acknowledgment dialog

When you select a Quality Hold part and click Next, the app shows an acknowledgment dialog. There are two acknowledgments — you must confirm both before the wizard can continue.

Read the entire dialog before clicking — it shows the Part ID, material type, and required actions
⚠️

QUALITY HOLD — Immediate Actions Required

The dialog displays the following required steps:

  • Contact Quality NOW
  • Quality MUST inspect and accept this load
  • DO NOT sign any paperwork until Quality accepts

You will be asked to confirm twice before saving.

What each button does

Button What it does
I Understand — Will Contact Quality Confirms you have read the notice and will contact the Quality team. Click this to advance to Acknowledgment 2 of 2.
Cancel Entry Cancels the current selection and returns to the parts table so you can choose a different part or stop the transaction.
🚫
Do not sign delivery paperwork for a Quality Hold part until the Quality team has physically inspected and accepted the load. Clicking "I Understand" in the app does not replace this physical inspection.

Screen Navigation

The bottom toolbar of the PO Entry screen contains three controls. Here is what each one does:

Control Location What it does
Mode Selection Bottom-left Returns you to the Mode Selection screen. Any unsaved input may be cleared.
Clear Label Data Bottom-left (next to Mode Selection) Clears the currently selected part and resets the form so you can start over. Appears in red when a Quality Hold part is selected — a reminder that clearing will remove that selection.
Next › Bottom-center Advances to the next wizard step. Only becomes active (clickable blue) when a part has been selected and any required Quality Hold acknowledgments have been completed.
ℹ️
Keyboard tip: Press Enter after typing a PO number or Part ID to trigger Load PO / Look Up Part without reaching for the mouse.

Common Questions

I typed the PO number but the parts list is empty. What's wrong?

Check the following:

  • Make sure you clicked Load PO after typing the number.
  • Verify the PO number is correct — check for extra spaces or typos.
  • The PO Status may show Closed, meaning all lines have been fully received and no open lines remain.
  • If the status shows an error, contact your supervisor or IT support.
The "Next" button is greyed out even though I selected a row.
The most common reason is that the selected part is on Quality Hold. Click the row, then look for the Quality Hold acknowledgment dialog. You must click I Understand — Will Contact Quality on both acknowledgment screens before Next becomes active.
There are two parts on the PO — how do I know which one to pick?
Match the Part ID and Description in the table to the part label on the physical delivery. If you are unsure, compare the Line # to the PO document. When in doubt, check with your supervisor before selecting.
I accidentally selected the wrong part. How do I clear it?
Click Clear Label Data in the bottom-left toolbar. This resets your selection and lets you choose a different row from the Available Parts table.
What does "Partially Received" mean in the PO Status?
It means at least one line on this PO has already been received, but some quantity is still outstanding. This is normal — the Remaining column in the parts table shows exactly how much is still expected.
The Part ID field shows "Package Type: Skids" — can I change this?
The package type is automatically detected from the part's InforVisual configuration. It cannot be changed on this screen. If the package type appears wrong, contact your supervisor — the part may need to be reconfigured in InforVisual.
Can I switch from Non-PO back to PO Entry?
Yes. Click Switch to PO Entry at any time before clicking Next. Any Part ID you have typed will be cleared when you switch modes.
A dialog appeared saying "No exact part match was found". What do I do?

That is the Smart Part Search dialog. It opens automatically when you type a Part ID that isn't an exact match in InforVisual. The app found similar parts and is asking you to pick the right one.

Click the row that matches the part label on the physical delivery, then click Select. If none of the suggestions look correct, click Cancel and double-check the Part ID from the label. See Smart Part Search for a complete walkthrough.
