Highlights

What is new in 4.5.0

Every lot gets its own shape and color

A new Heat / Lot Management tool in Ship/Rec Tools gives each heat number and supplier a permanent shape and color. The marking is chosen once, then carried onto every receipt line, history row, and reprint for that lot.

The board shows the real next run

Associated Parts now shows the job that still needs the material instead of an old finished job, uses the required date when no schedule exists, and labels each row Currently Running, Overdue, Due Today, or Past Job. Inbound coil orders that carry no warehouse appear again.

Current Total updates as you type

On the Receiving Enter Weight & Quantity step, each box now accepts digits only and the running Current Total moves on every keystroke instead of waiting for you to leave the box.

What changed

Details of this release

New Heat / Lot Management tool

Ship/Rec Tools gained a Heat / Lot Management tool. Every unique heat number and supplier is given one shape and one color the first time it is seen, and that marking is kept forever. The same lot number from two different suppliers receives two different markings, so they never look alike.

Search by heat or lot number to see existing markings. Select a marking to change its shape or color; a reason is required, and the change is recorded with who made it and when. Any marking can also be locked so later maintenance cannot change it.

Two maintenance actions are available from the tool:

  • Fill unmarked lots works on the selected part number. It previews how many receiving queue and history rows have no marking, then fills only those rows.
  • Update All Lots previews and then rewrites the marking on rows that already carry one, for the case where a lot number was corrected after labels were printed. Rows with no marking are left alone, and locked lots are never changed.
Markings appear while you receive

The Receiving Heat/Lot step now shows the marking for the lot you entered, so the shape and color can be checked before the receipt is saved. The marking is stored with the receipt and travels through the queue, into history, and out again on a reprint.

Lot marking settings

Core Settings gained a Heat / Lot Management page. It controls whether markings are used at all, chooses what happens when two lots would collide, sets whether a marking reads shape first or color first, and limits the shapes and colors on offer.

The page also holds the list of part numbers that receive markings. The list starts empty, so nothing changes until a part is added. Parts are added by typing a part number and choosing it from the match list, not by free text.

Associated Parts shows the job that is actually next

The board used to treat "the date has passed" as "the job is finished", so a closed job from last year could be shown as the next run while a work order released that morning was hidden. Ranking is now based on whether the work order and its material requirement are still open:

  • A job running right now comes first, then an open job that is past due, then an open job due soonest.
  • Finished jobs are kept only as a fallback when nothing is open, and are labeled Past Job.
  • An open job that has blown its date is labeled Overdue, and a job due today is labeled Due Today.
Jobs without a schedule now show a real date

Most open material requirements in Infor Visual have no operation schedule and no work-order schedule, so the card said No scheduled date for almost every open job. The board now falls back to the requirement's required date, then the work order's want date, and shows the best available date. Only released and open work orders are counted as demand; unreleased master planning records and quotes no longer appear.

Inbound coil orders appear again

Coil purchase orders are usually written without a warehouse because the receiving location decides where the coil lands. The board used to require a warehouse match, so those orders were dropped and the Incoming Material section stayed empty. Inbound material is no longer filtered by warehouse, and the large coil balances now show with their dates.

Blanket orders also show a date now. Previously a blanket release with a balance but no receipt ever shown no date at all; it now uses its promise or requested date while keeping the last-received date as history.

Delivery dates say whether they are confirmed or requested

The Incoming Material section now shows an Expected delivery block with two clearly worded lines: Confirmed when the date is a promise from the purchase order, and Requested when it is an internal desired-receive date. A past date with quantity still outstanding is marked overdue, and an order with no delivery date at all says No delivery date on the PO instead of showing the day the order was written.

The Look Ahead selector is now called Highlight window. It still shows everything, but dates beyond the chosen window are marked so the window length is not mistaken for a filter.

Weight and quantity boxes update Current Total while typing

Each box on the Enter Weight & Quantity step now accepts digits only, so expressions and stray characters cannot be entered. The value is applied on every keystroke, which means Current Total updates as you type rather than after you click away or press Enter. Focus on step entry lands in the first box.

In-app help for lot marking

A new help article covers the Heat / Lot Management tool, including how markings are chosen, how to change or lock one, and what the two maintenance actions do. It is available from the help button on the tool page.

Why it matters

What this means on the floor

Lots are easier to tell apart

Two coils with similar heat numbers no longer look the same. The shape and color travel with the receipt, so the marking you check at the Heat/Lot step is the marking you see later in history and on a reprint.

The board can be trusted for scheduling

A card that shows a next run now means a work order that still needs material. Overdue and Due Today labels point at the jobs that need attention, and the coil balances that were invisible are back on the card.

Fewer corrections while receiving

The running total is visible while you key weights, so a mis-typed digit is caught before you leave the box.

What you need to do

Set up lot marking when you are ready

No action is required after this update.

  • The new lot-marking tables and stored procedures are included in the installation package; there is nothing to run by hand.
  • Markings stay off for every part until a part number is added to the list in Core Settings → Heat / Lot Management. Add parts there when your team is ready to start using markings.
  • Existing receipts, history, and preferences carry over unchanged.

Need help?

Reporting a problem

If a lot shows a marking you did not expect, a marking cannot be changed, the Availability Board shows a job that looks wrong, inbound material is missing from a card, or Current Total does not follow your typing, contact your supervisor or the MTM IT support team and include the version number shown at the top of this page.
